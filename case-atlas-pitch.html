You don't pick music.
You get thrown.

ATLAS is a music time machine that throws you into the sound of a place and an era.

The premise

Another app asking what you want to hear?

Choosing is work. ATLAS starts with a place and an era, then lets the music find you.

The space

Different tools. Different kinds of discovery.

Radiooooo, Every Noise, Spotify and ATLAS approach discovery from different directions. The comparison will make the product distinction explicit here.

Three moves. No need to choose.

  • Spin the planet and let go. It keeps turning, then a spring pulls the nearest city under the crosshair — carrying your finger's speed, so there's no seam between throw and landing.

  • Ten decades on a drum. The first version was a glass capsule, and people read it as a button. Now the whole drum is what you turn, and the year lights up as it slides under the glass.

  • A place & era at once. Time always leads: every decade is reachable, and a city with nothing recorded then simply leaves the globe.

The loop

A find becomes a reason to come back.

Throw → listen → refine → like → return. This section will show the real product loop with ATLAS screens.

Why it fits

The gesture is ours. The catalog is theirs.

ATLAS combines a playful discovery mechanic with the depth of a streaming service.

How it’s made

A system built around motion, sound and place.

Design system, 3D globe, physical controls and one shared core across mobile and desktop.
